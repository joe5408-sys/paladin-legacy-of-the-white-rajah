# Game Design — Paladin: Legacy of the White Rajah

> The full authoritative spec is in `MASTER_PROMPT.md`. This file is a quick reference.

**Genre:** story-driven HD-2D fantasy RPG (3D environments, 2D pixel sprites).
**Setting:** a fictional alternate modern-day Sarawak where the Brooke dynasty survived as a constitutional monarchy. This is fiction: it is not real Malaysian or Sarawakian constitutional history. The Paladin order and its mythology are original and invented.
**Theme:** *The Crown remembers its heroes. History remembers its traitors. But who decides which is which?*

## Prologue — Chapter 0: The Forgotten Heir
1. **Arrival:** Adrian Vale (24) returns to Sungai Awan, a fictional riverside settlement near Kapit. Objective: find the family home.
2. **The Family Secret:** Aunt Mara reveals the family served the White Rajah and was accused of treason.
3. **The Awakening:** the heirloom reacts and reveals a symbol and a hidden chamber.
4. **First Battle:** the Covenant Guardian, a turn-based tutorial fight.
5. **Revelation:** *"The oath was never sworn to the Crown alone."*
6. **Royal Attention:** Daniel Rengas (royal security) warns that the awakening was detected and points toward Kuching. → CHAPTER 0 COMPLETE.

## Areas
- **A — Village Square** (built): riverside boardwalk, Kedai Runcit, Klinik Desa, Vale family home on stilts.
- **B — Ancestral Home** interior (Phase 3).
- **C — Ancient Chamber** battle arena (Phase 4).

## Combat (Phase 4) — starting balance
Player HP 30 · Guardian HP 24 · Attack 6 · Radiant Strike 10 (2 uses) · Guardian hit 4 · Defend halves the next hit.

## Cultural guardrails
Represent Sarawak's multicultural society respectfully; don't present invented sacred traditions as authentic; never link supernatural corruption to any real ethnic or religious group.
